The best "workflow" is to try shoes in a shop, understand which model and size is best, and then try to find them on Vinted. Usually at least 20% lower, even for "new with tags". Haggling is recommended!

## Running shoes (size 9UK, 43EU)
I generally prefer Salomon shoes here, both for road and for trail. I haven't really tried other brands so far to be fair, since they fit me quite well and I know the sizing. Might try in the future and update. 
- Salomon Aero Blaze 3 Grvl GTX (on trails only)
- Salomon Glide Max (for road only)
- Salomon Sense Ride 5 (nice and light, can do both road and trails)
- Salomon Speedcross 4 (ran a lot with them - now retired)

## Climbing shoes (size 7.5UK, 41EU)
Here, I almost exclusively use La Sportiva shoes. I've tried the Scarpa Instinct VS and didn't love them. 
- La Sportiva Solution (probably my favourite ones, have owned several pairs over the years)
- La Sportiva Skwama (ok, a bit too roomy for me at the same size as the Solutions)
- La Sportiva Skwama Lite (very nice fit out of the box, not too aggressive, good all-rounder at a more reasonable price)
- La Sportiva Theory (quite soft, not amazing for small footholds)

## Mountaineering boots (size 9.5UK, 43-44EU)
So far I've only owned one pair:
- Scarpa Charmoz OD
