## Smartwatches
- My current one is the Garmin Fenix 7X Pro Solar. I got it in October 2023 for the [[ebc2023|EBC trek]]. It's awesome and I take it off only when I charge it (which is not very often, because its battery is so good!). I track sleep, stress, running, cycling, football, hikes, mountaineering, essentially all activities apart from climbing and lifting, with it. I honestly think there's nothing really missing from it, except potentially maybe InReach ability, if it didn't come at a battery cost. 
- My previous one was the Garmin Fenix 6. I only changed it so that I could have the larger battery and the solar option for the trek, since charging would be tricky in the teahouses. Gave it to my brother and it still works great.
- First smartwatch was the Garmin Vivoactive 3. Relatively limited, but a great first watch to start tracking running with.
