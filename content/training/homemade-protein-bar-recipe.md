It's a super easy and fast process. 

![Two thick homemade protein bars on a black plate, glossy dark chocolate tops flecked with peanut, dense oaty crumb studded with blueberries in the cut face](attachments/protein_bars.jpg)

## Ingredients
- 280g Whey Protein
- 150g Oats
- 35g Cocoa Powder
- 60g Raisins (optional)
- 100g Honey
- 40g Peanut Butter (smooth)
- Oat Milk (by eye)
- Blueberries (optional, a handful)

## Process
1. Preheat oven at ~150deg Celsius
2. Get a glass oven dish, line with baking paper (and potentially get a foil sheet cover)
3. In a big bowl, add the protein powder, the oats, the cocoa powder, and the raisins. Mix well with a big wooden spoon
4. In a small microwaveable container, add the honey and the peanut butter. Microwave for 10-15sec. Mix with a spoon
5. Add the honey-peanut butter mix into the big bowl of powders and mix well
6. Add the milk, enough to make everything mix well and be homogeneous, not dripping, not dry. (you'll get a feel for this after the first couple of times)
7. Mix in the blueberries too if you feel like it. 
8. Put the mix in the oven dish thing, spread evenly (I don't make them too thin, a bit like brownies, so get an appropriately-sized dish).
9. I cover the dish with foil, leave in the oven for ~14min, remove the foil and leave for another ~2.5min. This gives them the consistency I enjoy. 
10. Cut with a pizza cutter. I cut into 10 pieces, and that corresponds to ~25g protein with ~250kcal.
