Consistency trumps almost all the other principles. It's not a one-off, very intense workout that will improve us, but the consistent application of training stimulus after stimulus year week after week, year after year.

Consistency can be boring by definition, because it means repeating a certain action over time. A certain amount of tolerance to boredom is necessary in order to adhere to any training/nutrition plan long enough to see real effects. 

There is a tension with novelty/exploration and knowing when to quit something that isn't working. It's a tricky line to walk and to decide between [[exploration-vs-exploitation|exploration and exploitation]].
