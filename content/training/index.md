I train across several disciplines at once, mainly climbing (mostly bouldering, but also sport, trad, winter climbing), running (a big chunk of it being trail running), weight lifting (mainly big 3 + climbing-specific), and football. I try to maintain a high enough level across all of them, in order to ensure I remain mediocre haha. 

This section will have posts related to:
- **Logs** — periodic write-ups of training blocks, projects, and the occasional PR.
- **Analyses** — what the multi-year biometrics say about autonomic state, body weight coupling, strength and CNS, VO2max, sleep, perturbation events. Methodology and headline findings, with the most sensitive day-by-day data probably kept private.
- **Concepts** — things I've worked out about the response of my own body to training and nutrition, my decision-making process about how I train etc, injury prevention and rehabilitation, concurrent training, and other similar lessons I've learned either from studying my own data or from other sources (which I'll try to cite to the best of my ability).

## Operating principles

I try to adhere to the fundamental [[Training Principles|training principles]]. As an adjunct, I try to use data from my garmin watch about sleep, recovery, stress, etc. The data does not fully dictate my training, but sometimes it does inform my decisions. I try to stay in touch with my own subjective feelings about recovery and so on.
