- [[side-projects/trail-profile|Trail Profile]] — drop a batch of your own runs and get a read on your engine, climbing, descending, durability and intensity mix, with the tool's own accuracy stated.
- [[side-projects/why-consistency-wins|Why consistency wins]] — an interactive toy of stimulus, fatigue, supercompensation and decay, and what it says about missing sessions, spacing them, and doing too much.
- [[side-projects/the-clock-not-the-board|The clock, not the board]] — six years of my blitz games through Stockfish, expecting a tactical hole and finding that I blunder most on the moves I think longest about.
