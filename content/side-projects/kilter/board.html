Model and data: the write-up. Hold difficulty is the community grade of the climbs a hold appears in (no hold was labelled); the additive model was scored strictly by climb; the sequence model is hand-tuned and never fitted to grades. Grade-relative comparisons use the 40° population, so the style analysis runs at your most-climbed angle and is exact only at 40°.
