## The route

Out of the Cabane de Moiry heading south-east then east onto the glacier, up to the **Col du Pigne (3140 m)**, then south along the **NW ridge** to the summit. Descent by the same line — the south-west glacier descent is heavily crevassed and not worth it without a full glacier kit and a reason.

The graded crux is a short section of exposed scrambling on the ridge with loose blocks in it. Helmet on from the col.

## What actually happened

Away from the hut at 05:40. Warm enough to climb in a t-shirt. We stayed roped but barely placed anything — the ridge is a scramble, not a pitch.

The lesson of the day was the **descent**, not the ascent. Coming down the wet rocky slabs in crampons felt considerably sketchier than "F" suggests, and I fumbled the crampon fitting under time pressure. That theme — that the *down* and the *mixed ground* are the sharp end, not the fitness — repeated on every peak of the trip.
