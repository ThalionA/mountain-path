What a sixteen-day, self-guided Alpine trip actually costs, for two people driving from the UK. Numbers are the real ones from [[alps2026|Alps 2026]], reconciled against the card export after we got home, not the plan.

**Headline: £3,093 for two people — £1,547 each.** Budget was £2,491–2,943, so about **£150 over the top of the range**. For sixteen days including five 3000–4200 m objectives, two 4000ers, seven nights in mountain huts and 2,400 km of driving, that felt like value.

## The three lessons

1. **Food was the big miss.** £626 against a plan of £130–215 — the single worst estimate in the budget. The assumption that "half-board covers most dinners" collapsed against a self-catering camping base: repeated supermarket shops in three countries, plus valley restaurant meals on rest days. Budget food as if the huts feed you on hut nights only, and as if every other day is a normal holiday.
2. **Fuel ran over.** ~2,400 km round trip at a real-world 52 mpg is roughly 130 litres of diesel, but across UK, French, Swiss and Italian pumps. Driving still won on gear capacity — no airline restrictions on sharp objects or lithium batteries, and no re-booking penalty for extending.
3. **Alpine insurance came in under plan, via a side door.** Two **Austrian Alpine Club memberships at £57.50 each** covered worldwide alpine rescue and repatriation across both Switzerland and Italy, replacing the separate REGA membership plus travel policy the budget had assumed. £115 total against £97–147 planned, for better cover.

## Not in these numbers

- **Guiding: £0.** Self-guided throughout, which is the biggest single cost lever on a trip like this.
- **A tandem paragliding flight** — a personal gift, not a shared trip cost, so deliberately excluded.
- **The pool and spa day** at St Luc, which turned out to be free with the campsite guest card.
- **Gear bought before departure** — that belongs to the kit, not to the trip.

> [!note] Currencies
> The trip crossed GBP, CHF and EUR. Everything above is converted to sterling at rates locked at planning time (2026-05-06) so that budget and actual stay comparable.
