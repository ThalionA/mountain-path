## The route

From the **Cabane de Tracuit**, south-east across the **Turtmanngletscher** to the saddle near pt. 3543, then south-east up broad snow slopes to a short steep snow ridge and the summit.

Two things to respect. The glacier is **well crevassed**, and there is a short steep step — the *raidillon* — at around 4120 m below the summit col. In fog the glacier plateau is genuinely featureless, so set a summit bearing at the hut before leaving.

## What actually happened

A rude 03:20 wake-up in a full and noisy hut. Away by about 04:50.

The crux was a **big crevasse crossing** — holes dropping away to blackness, stepping across snow bridges. We passed a lot of teams on the way up and I swapped to the front to set a pace that worked for both of us. Summit in a shade under three hours; I felt strong on the steep upper slope, and altitude never became the limiting factor it might have been.

The honest note from the day: *more sketched out by the people than by the mountain*. Inexperienced parties, ropes without brake knots, the real prospect of someone else's fall becoming our problem on a crevasse field.

Descent to the hut was fine, if hot. The **1600 m down to Zinal** afterwards took 2 h 35 and left both of us with sore knees — the price of doing the peak as a single push and sleeping in the valley.
