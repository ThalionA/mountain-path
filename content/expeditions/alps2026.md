My first proper Alpine expedition: sixteen days in the Pennine Alps, self-guided as a two-person rope team. The objective was modest by design — a handful of routes in the **F / PD- / PD** range to build mountaineering experience on real glaciated terrain rather than chase a hard summit.

The shape of it was a ladder. Start in the Val d'Anniviers with two low-grade glaciated peaks out of the Cabane de Moiry, use them to acclimatise and rehearse the skills, then step up to a first 4000er on the Bishorn, then cross to the Italian side of Monte Rosa for the high ground.

**Four summits from five.** Pigne de la Lé, Pointes de Mourti, Bishorn and Vincent Pyramid all went. Signalkuppe (4554 m), the apex, did not — we came down instead of grinding out a long crevassed glacier day on tired legs. Not lost so much as traded.

## Day by day

| Date | Where | What happened | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fri 19 – Sat 20 Jun | UK → Val d'Anniviers | Eurotunnel, then ~700 km across France and Switzerland to Vissoie | Drove rather than flew — gear capacity, no airline restrictions. Camp as valley base |
| Sun 21 Jun | Vissoie / Grimentz | Valley acclimatisation hike and gear shakedown | ~600 m, 3–4 h. Boots and crampons checked in anger |
| Mon 22 Jun | Lac de Moiry → Cabane de Moiry (2825 m) | Approach walk to the hut | +700 m, ~2 h. Pack far too heavy. Afternoon recon of the Pigne's lower route |
| Tue 23 Jun | **Pigne de la Lé (3396 m)** — F/PD- | ✅ **First Alpine summit.** Out 05:40, NNW ridge from the Col du Pigne | ~6h40 hut to hut. Warm enough for a t-shirt. The *descent* was the sketchy part — crampons on wet slabs |
| Wed 24 Jun | **Pointes de Mourti (3564 m)** — PD | ✅ **Summit**, and the long day of the trip. Moiry glacier, then the NE ridge | 10h40 hut to hut. Extended along the ridge toward the Dent des Rosses, two rappels to get off it. Back across soft glacier snow later than the plan allowed, then down to the dam ahead of a storm |
| Thu 25 Jun | Val d'Anniviers | Rest day — pool and spa at St Luc | Deliberate recovery after Mourti |
| Fri 26 Jun | Crans-Montana, then Zinal → Cabane de Tracuit (3256 m) | Tandem paraglide in the morning; hut approach in the afternoon | +1600 m in under 4h10. Busy, basic hut |
| Sat 27 Jun | **Bishorn (4153 m)** — F | ✅ **First 4000er.** 03:20 wake-up, away by 04:50, up the Turtmann glacier | 5h24 hut–summit–hut. Crux was a big crevasse crossing on snow bridges. Then 1600 m down to Zinal — more unnerved by the crowds than by the mountain |
| Sun 28 Jun | Switzerland → Italy | Broke camp, drove the Simplon Pass to Gressoney-la-Trinité | Transit day |
| Mon 29 Jun | Stafal → Rifugio Gnifetti (3647 m) | Cable cars to Punta Indren, then walked in ahead of a forecast storm | Hut full at ~160 people. Long evening of wondering why one does this at all |
| Tue 30 Jun | **Vincent Pyramid (4215 m)** — PD | ✅ **Second 4000er**, via a technical SSW→SE ridge variant rather than the glacier voie normale | Out 05:30, summit in ~5h15, hut at 6h30. Crampons kept on for mixed snow, ice and rock — the hardest ground of the trip. Punta Giordani deliberately cut with cloud building |
| Wed 1 Jul | Signalkuppe / Punta Gnifetti (4554 m) | ✗ **Not attempted** — descended to the valley by choice | Both of us had had our fill. The turnaround discipline that worked all trip applied to the apex too |
| Thu 2 – Fri 3 Jul | Italy → UK | Simplon, France, Eurotunnel, home | — |
