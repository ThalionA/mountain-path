Maybe I'm very late to the party, but while researching and preparing for the [[alps2026|Alps 2026]] trip, I found out about .gpx files. These are files that you can find online, e.g. at [Wikiloc \| Trails of the World](https://www.wikiloc.com), which describe the GPS coordinates of an entire route. You can export them yourself from previous routes you have recorded with your Garmin watch, for example, and then share them. You can download them and import them to Garmin Connect (and then send to your watch), or import them into another app on your phone like [outdooractive.com • Europe's largest outdoor platform \| Outdooractive](https://www.outdooractive.com/en/).
