This is really an excellent book. Covers everything from basic physiology of endurance, strength, altitude adaptations and so on, to practical recommendations for training, example training programs from different expeditions of the authors and so on. Highly recommended! At some point, I will do a full breakdown of all the lessons I learned from it.
