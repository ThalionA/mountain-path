---
title: Availability bias
aliases:
  - "availability bias"
---

Part of the various [[cognitive-biases|cognitive biases]]. 
When asked to make a judgement of frequency or probability of an event, humans substitute this with an easier question of "how easily do instances of this kind of event come to mind?". 

Potentially helpful during evolutionary times where better-remembered events are the ones more frequently encountered, unhelpful now because representation in the news (and therefore, memory) is not directly tied to frequency.
