# Understanding — YouTube restart system

*Current as of 2026-07-06.*

## Goal
Restart uploads to @theoamvr after an 8-month gap (last video: Climbing Benchmarks, Nov 2025). Two aims, in order: **ship consistently with a low bar**, and **grow the audience** (titles/thumbnails/cadence matter, polish does not).

## Content
Same channel identity as before: climbing/alpinism footage, training content, talking-head/explainer (neuroscience, book club). Existing back-catalogue of 16 videos and the ideas library in MountainMind/Blog-Youtube remain the source of material.

## Diagnosed bottlenecks (all four confirmed)
1. Editing scope ballooned (Benchmarks set a 25-min precedent).
2. Postdoc workload — pipeline must fit small time slots.
3. Restart inertia — cold workflow, cold Premiere skills.
4. Decision paralysis — 80-item ideas menu, no queue.

## Decisions
- **Home**: MountainMind/Blog-Youtube stays the system's home. The vault's blanket "archived / read-only" status gets a carve-out for this folder; global CLAUDE.md and MountainMind CLAUDE.md updated accordingly. (`API keys/` remains untouchable.)
- **Cadence**: monthly — one video per calendar month, deliberately under the old 2×/month pace.
- **Scope control**: no hard cap. Instead, a predict-style mirror: each video's note records *estimated* edit sessions before the edit starts and *actual* after publish. Creep becomes visible, not policed.
- **Queue rule**: rolling NEXT-UP 3 in the pipeline doc. When one publishes, promote exactly one idea from the big list. Never face the full menu mid-month.
- **Current queue**: ① How to identify your weaknesses (follows on from Benchmarks — gap reads as continuity), ② Rock Warrior's Way (book club, safety video for a busy month), ③ Motor learning series intro.
- **Ritual**: none. Pull-based — Theo opens PIPELINE.md when he has time; the doc's job is to make the single next action obvious in under a minute.
- **Publish checklist** includes creating the video's page in MountainPath `content/youtube/` (existing pattern) and ticking the idea ✅ in Content ideas.md.

## Won't-Do (considered and excluded)
- Hard length/edit-session caps (chose awareness + logging instead).
- Scheduled Claude check-ins or calendar-block rituals (chose pull-based).
- Migrating the system to PersonalWiki/ExpeditionVault/a fresh vault.
- Fixed "season of 6" planning; returning straight to 2×/month.
- Upload automation, Shorts strategy — never in scope this round.
