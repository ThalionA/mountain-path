FULL REHAB SCHEDULE (written up): https://thaliona.github.io/mountain-path/training/brachialis-rehab-schedule

The short version:
▸ Phase 1 (≈wk 1–4): banded reverse curls, palm down — 2–3 sets of 15–20, twice a day, plus one set before every climb
▸ Phase 2 (≈wk 4–10): dumbbell reverse curls 3×8–12 with slow lowering, 2–3×/week; add hammer curls once comfortable
▸ Phase 3 (wk 10+): pullups → weighted pullups, small jumps
▸ Throughout: keep climbing 2–3 grades below your max, warm up thoroughly, avoid deep lock-offs and big explosive moves early
▸ Pain rule: up to 3/10 that settles by next morning = carry on; worse = don't progress / step back
Not medical advice — get assessed first (thanks again to physio Phil Mack).
